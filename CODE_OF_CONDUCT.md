# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Welcome different perspectives, give actionable feedback, and focus disagreement on ideas and behavior rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with appropriate confidentiality. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior violates this standard.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
